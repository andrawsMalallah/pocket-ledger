This policy describes the Penny mobile app and this download page.

Financial records stay on your device

The app stores expenses, salary records, savings adjustments, categories, budgets, and settings locally. The ledger contents are encrypted before being written to SQLite; the encryption key is held in the operating system’s secure storage. The app does not send financial records to a server. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, or automatic cloud synchronization.

Device authentication

Penny uses your phone’s authentication system for fingerprint, face recognition, or device screen-lock verification. It does not receive or store your fingerprint or face data. The app locks when it enters the background.

Exports and backups

Encrypted backups are protected by the password you choose. CSV exports are not encrypted. You choose the destination using the system file picker; choosing a cloud provider there may upload the exported file to that provider. Exported files are not removed when you clear the app’s data.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete them, reset the ledger, or remove the app. System backup exclusion settings are configured for the app’s financial database. Export a backup before uninstalling, changing phones, or making major changes. A lost password cannot be recovered by the app’s developer.

This website

This static website does not use analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses and access logs to serve the page and download. Downloading the app does not share your ledger with this website.

Permissions

The Android release requests biometric authentication. Importing and exporting use the system document picker; the app does not request broad access to your files, contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Security scope

Local encryption and device authentication protect ordinary device use. They cannot guarantee protection on a compromised operating system. Keep your phone updated and your exported backups private.
