How to install
  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If prompted, allow your browser to install this app.
  3. Install and open Pocket Ledger. Authenticate with your device, choose your currency in Settings, and add your salary.
  4. For future updates, install over the existing app. Back up first; do not uninstall.
